Plan this round yourself after inspecting the student history and relevant corpus. Return a Curriculum object.
lessons: any number of tasks, in your chosen order, each with a unique id. Choose cpt for teaching prose or sft for QA-as-text; you can choose all of either, or none for a reading/review/diagnostic round. sources lists the document spans you choose (start/length in original file characters; length=0 means the rest). An empty sources list means a teacher-authored exercise. concept labels the subject, prompt is the task/question label, and student_prompt is EXACTLY what the student should see. Include or omit source context as you judge useful; the engine will not add text. Explain the teaching reason.
readings: raw corpus spans to train on; these are explicit selections, not automatically all lesson sources.
replay: explicitly chosen historical lesson references {round_id, lesson_id}, from any campaign or age. The immutable teacher lesson is replayed as recorded. You may instead create a new lesson to revise older teaching.
token_mix: teacher/corpus/replay shares summing to 1; zero shares are allowed. If a stream is absent, available shares are renormalized and reported. Choose train_epochs>=0. train_epochs=0 explicitly keeps the weights unchanged, even if you prepare lessons/readings for future use. With positive train_epochs, config train_steps=0 AUTOMATICALLY computes updates from the prepared tokens and your chosen passes; it does not disable training. A positive operator train_steps overrides that automatic update count only when you enable training. Avoid inadvertent repetition. The worker executes these fields; express your training decision here rather than only in notes.
evaluation_instructions: decide the purpose, breadth, question types, difficulty and extent of the post-training diagnostic. The evaluation author can adapt after inspecting this round. notes: record why this plan fits the student.
All lists may be empty for a diagnostic-only round. The engine must not label that as weight training. Read the previous teaching strategy and choose freely; do not use a fixed alternating CPT/QA pattern or fixed question count.
