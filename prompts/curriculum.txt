lessons: any number of tasks, in your chosen order, each with a unique id. Within the unified Mid-training recipe, kind=cpt labels teaching prose and kind=sft labels QA supervision. These are material labels, not separate training modes; you can choose any composition, all of either, or none for a reading/review/diagnostic round. sources lists the document spans you choose (start/length in original file characters; length=0 means the rest). An empty sources list means a teacher-authored exercise. concept labels the subject, prompt is the task/question label, and student_prompt is your exact student content. student_format=campaign uses config_hints.student_format: raw_text continues it literally; chat_template wraps it as one native no-thinking user message. readings: raw corpus spans to train on; these are explicit selections, not automatically all lesson sources.

You may instead create a new lesson to revise older teaching.
comparison_round_id: empty normally. To investigate checkpoint changes, you may select one completed historical round with a different checkpoint stored in this workspace's runs. The worker verifies its immutable training artifact and runs the same teacher-authored lesson content on the current and reference checkpoints using each saved interface (old artifacts default to raw_text), sequentially within one shared stage timeout. Nonempty lessons are required. Each lesson retains its current student answer and a separate comparison object with the reference round, checkpoint, artifact and generation evidence; both are available to revision and reflection. This neither replaces the current checkpoint nor inserts reference answers into training or assessment. You decide prompts, material, passes and interpretation; train_epochs=0 makes the comparison weight-preserving. comparison_checkpoint defaults to output. Set it to input to compare the selected completed training round's recorded input: its model_before must match the immutable training artifact's parent. A local checkpoint must belong to this workspace's runs, match a completed parent round's immutable training artifact, and pass manifest and file-hash verification. A pinned local Hub snapshot instead requires content-addressed cache-blob verification. The worker freezes file hashes at selection and rechecks them before generation. Arbitrary paths and local inputs without completed parent provenance are unsupported. Snapshot checks establish recorded path and current byte integrity, not a retroactive hash of the original training input. This is evidence only; no restoration or score gate is implied.
token_mix: teacher/corpus/replay shares summing to 1; zero shares are allowed. For a diagnostic round with train_epochs=0, all three shares may instead be 0, leaving no prepared training target tokens. A sum of 1 with train_epochs=0 preserves prepared token samples without training on them. If a stream is absent, available shares are renormalized and reported. Choose train_epochs>=0. train_epochs=0 explicitly keeps the weights unchanged, even if you prepare lessons/readings for future use. With positive train_epochs, config train_steps=0 AUTOMATICALLY computes updates from the prepared tokens and your chosen passes; it does not disable training. A positive operator train_steps overrides that automatic update count only when you enable training. Choose repetition deliberately and explain its teaching purpose. The worker executes these fields; express your training decision here rather than only in notes.

All lists may be empty for a diagnostic-only round. The engine must not label that as weight training. Read the previous teaching strategy and choose freely; do not use a fixed material alternation or fixed question count.
scoring_round_ids: optionally select completed historical rounds that actually trained. The worker scores their exact frozen token samples on each round's verified before/after checkpoints, recording FP32 and (on CUDA) BF16-autocast target/EOS probabilities and token loss. Exact untruncated recorded prompt prefixes allow separate prompt and continuation loss; other samples remain unassigned. This runs in draft before current prompts, sharing its model-execution timeout, and is saved as historical_scoring in the draft artifact and reflection input. You can read that artifact for earlier inspection. It never retokenizes training text, generates replacement answers, updates weights, changes the current checkpoint, or supplies a learning gate. Empty disables scoring. Select train_epochs=0 for an entirely weight-preserving diagnostic round; scoring itself never trains. All historical rounds remain selectable without a recency cutoff, subject to immutable provenance and execution budgets.

task.previous_learning_work reports the latest completed round's actual token exposure, optimizer updates, inner training time, stage walltime and current checkpoint bytes. Use it when choosing material and train_epochs: round count is not training dose, and a small one-pass dataset can pay a large fixed authoring/loading/saving/review cost for one short update. Explain the chosen dose relative to the previous observation in notes. More material or additional passes can amortize that cost, but neither is required or automatically beneficial. Select a bounded learnability/transfer contrast when uncertainty warrants it, using compare_before evaluations or existing historical scoring. Distinguish trained-example recall, fresh transfer, retention and format; decide when prerequisite practice should reconnect to the campaign's subject. You retain all curriculum, repetition, assessment and continuation choices; no fixed minimum tokens, passes, mix or score applies.

Plan a coherent work package, rather than treating lessons_per_round as a ceiling. config_hints.workload_guidance records the operator's preference for useful training work. Same-checkpoint student prompts now run in bounded batches; execution batch size does not limit how many lessons you can choose. When fixed authoring/loading/saving costs dominate tiny updates, consider a materially larger set of relevant examples, explanations, selected readings and review, with useful variation and coverage. Repeating a handful of examples only to make the GPU busy is not new coverage. A small or diagnostic round remains your choice when its information is worth the cost; explain that purpose.

work_plan records your estimated_targets_per_pass (causal prediction targets including prompt/EOS, before train_epochs), material_strategy and dose_rationale. This is a teacher estimate, not a measurement or a required minimum. Use null only when an estimate is unavailable. The frozen preparation and measured update_work will be returned for comparison at reflection and next selection. Missing that estimate never blocks training.

The current mixture uses the first nonempty positive-share stream in teacher/corpus/replay order as its anchor. All available anchor targets are prepared once per pass; other streams fill the ratios relative to that amount. Therefore adding a large reading list while keeping a tiny teacher anchor and small corpus share can leave most selected reading targets unused. preparation.streams exposes available, prepared, unused and repeated target occurrences; these are not deduplicated content counts. Choose the material and shares together to express your intended coverage. Each dataset pass flushes its final partial update: a 500-target dataset trained twice is two 500-target updates, not two full tokens_per_update batches. Larger useful material and extra repetition are different interventions; decide deliberately.


Material Authors can generate additional teaching material under your plan. task.material_authors lists the frozen available authors, credential availability, request/output allowances and concurrency. You remain the only teaching authority. Set expansion_jobs=[] when delegation is not useful or credentials are unavailable. Otherwise choose jobs with a unique id, author_id, corrected seed_ids from this curriculum, optional reading_indices into your readings list, explicit instructions, expected_items and max_output_tokens. Jobs execute after your seed revisions. Different jobs can run concurrently; split by useful coverage, not a fixed author count. Each job receives only its declared seeds/readings and your plan notes. Its output is synthetic candidate material without a student attempt. Do not require a student rollout for every variant unless you choose those tasks separately.

All requested jobs must fit the provided execution allowance. max_output_tokens reserves API output including provider reasoning, which is not automatically training text. Decide output scope accordingly; authors may return fewer candidates, which is reported for your selection rather than automatically filled. Expected material counts are not learning outcomes. A later material_select request lets you inspect all candidates, accept exact IDs or entire immutable job batches, edit content, omit seeds, and choose final shares/passes. Full candidates and sources remain accessible in the read-only archive. Reduce repetitive manual authoring by delegating appropriate variation while keeping useful teacher-corrected seed examples.
